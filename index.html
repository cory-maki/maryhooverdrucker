A Decade in the Field

Producing events the audience remembers, not just attends.

For ten-plus years, Mary Hoover Drucker has worked at the intersection of travel and corporate event strategy — first on the supplier side of the tourism industry, then in agency project management at FIRST. The throughline has been the same throughout: programs that respect the attendee’s time and convert that respect into business outcomes for the client.

From advisor incentives at oceanfront resorts to product launches inside cosmetic flagships, Mary’s portfolio at FIRST Agency leans toward briefs where logistics, hospitality, and brand storytelling have to land at the same moment.
